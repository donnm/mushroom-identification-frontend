### Why become a member?

Fleinkontroll is run by Tryggere Ruspolitikk (Safer Drug Policy) - the organization behind Rusopplysningen. Everything we do rests on one principle: knowledge can save lives. As a member, you support a knowledge-based, humane drug policy that puts health and human rights first, instead of punishment and moralizing.

Your membership gives you:

- **Real influence:** Take part in your local chapter's activities, vote at annual meetings, and run for elected positions.
- **A democratic say:** Elect delegates to the national congress and help shape the organization's policies and priorities.
- **Updates that matter:** Receive our newsletter on what's happening, locally and nationally.
- **A direct impact on our work:** The number of paying members determines how much operating support we receive from the Norwegian Directorate of Health (Helsedirektoratet) - your membership helps keep services like Fleinkontroll running.
